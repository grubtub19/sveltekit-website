# A note about the blog

Since I wrote this website from scratch, I never implemented a particularly robust image hosting solution. Unfortunately, I completely filled up my Cloudflare Pages storage capacity with photos, forcing me to abandon writing blogs as a refactor would days of my life. The rest of my Japan blog will be written almost a year later.

Writing this blog helped me rekindle my relationship with myself. Free from the anxieties of my life in NYC, from the people who abandoned me, from the people I abandoned, and space to remember who I am. This blog is a love letter.

TBH, I remember being relieved that I didn't have to blog anymore. It was important to me, but I think it served its purpose, and the time I gained back lead me to some cool opportunities as you'll see :)
