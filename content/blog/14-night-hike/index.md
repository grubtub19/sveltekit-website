# Night Hike

I'd been trying to research shows to go, but the research is exhausting. Even ignoring the language barrier, there's only so much you can glean from the internet. That being said, I've been tapped-in to Japanese twitter since my study abroad, so I have at least some leads, and an event series called "Night Hike" caught my eye. Many illustrators I follow had been retweeting live drawing sessions that they do during shows, which the concept is super cool, and their production, promo materials, merch, etc. was clean. I knew nothing about the music lol.

I invited Vivi and Lily, but they had other plans, so I went by myself. It was super fun! I didn't quite understand the music. The various DJs had a decent range of sound, but there seemed to be certain dominant themes that appear regardless of the genre: a bit vocaloid sounding, tends toward upbeat, and like idk not exactly my taste in music for sure, but the experience was super novel, so I had a lot of fun!

# Smoking in Miyashita Park

One of my side-projects this trip has been learning Japanese cigarette brands, and scouring the streets for the rarer ones. What's harder tho is trying to find somewhere to legally smoke here. There's some dedicated smoking areas, but they can be difficult to find depending on the area.

I went on an adventure in Shibuya to find a smoking room specifically, and found one on a floor of Miyashita Park. Obviously didn't take any photos, but the idea is there's a cylinder in the middle that you tap your ashes and butts into. The vibes are very funny in there, everyone just gets their fix and leaves.

I was pretty drunk from the bar, so the cigs hit like crazy. I found a nice spot in the park to listen to my own music and stare off into the distance for an hour.
